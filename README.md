## About Me

I am a **B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering** undergraduate at **Newton School of Technology** with a strong interest in **Artificial Intelligence**, **Machine Learning**, and **Full-Stack Development**. I enjoy building intelligent applications, experimenting with modern AI workflows, and solving practical problems through clean software engineering.

- **Education:** B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering — Newton School of Technology
- **AI & ML Interests:** Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Agentic AI, and RAG Systems
- **Engineering Approach:** Combining full-stack web architectures with applied AI models to build end-to-end, real-world software
- **Mindset:** Learning continuously through hands-on projects, experimentation, and iterative development
